Our product principles

What we’re
building toward.

The principles guiding Wellyn’s development.

01Fewer products to compare.

The goal is a manageable set of options, selected around your needs, preferences and budget. What you already own should be part of that decision, too.

02A reason for each suggestion.

You should be able to see why a product is suggested, what its trade-offs are and what information is missing. We intend to make paid partnerships and affiliate links clear wherever they appear.

03Support after the purchase.

We’re planning guidance that helps you use the products you choose, keep track of what works and adjust as your needs change. A useful next step may be to keep what you have.
