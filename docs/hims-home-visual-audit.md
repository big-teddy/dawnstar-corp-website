# Hims homepage: direct browser review

2026-09-30. Follow-up to the founder’s clarification: the immediate application is Dawnstar’s company page and its Wellyn service imagery. Detailed product functionality belongs to later Wellyn development.

## Scope correction

The preceding team review read the Hims homepage and product pages, but the lead’s live visual review focused on About and the AI app page. It was not a section-by-section browser review of the main homepage. This follow-up fills that specific gap.

The live desktop version at [hims.com](https://www.hims.com/) was reviewed from the first screen through the bottom footer. Screenshots were captured at a natural approximately 1280 × 720 viewport. This is one observed version, not all responsive sizes, experiments or site pages.

## Coverage

| Area | Direct inspection |
| --- | --- |
| Home opening | Main promise, promotional cards, care-category shortcuts |
| Weight-loss chapter | Large product scene, horizontal product carousel, AI app scene, connected scale |
| Sexual-health chapter | Human photograph, product cards, goal links, testimonial carousel |
| Testosterone chapter | Family photograph and graphic overlay, product and phone support cards |
| Labs chapter | Human image with interface overlays, changing phone screen, animated information graphics |
| Closing | Clinical team, email form, app download and complete footer |
| Navigation | Drawer and Skin submenu |
| Interactions | Product carousel horizontal scroll, next testimonial, opening the initial weight-loss question |

The main homepage repeatedly alternates large physical objects, human scenes and software in use. Compact cards provide comparison between those scenes. Color changes organize care chapters; typography and rounded geometry remain consistent. Motion carries information: the Labs phone changes from results to an action plan as the reader progresses. These are observed presentation patterns, not an identification of the site’s animation framework.

## Connected pages

- [Skin care](https://www.hims.com/skin-care): opening portrait, concern-based navigation and the Skin Basics product display were visually inspected.
- [Everyday Moisturizer](https://www.hims.com/skin-care/moisturizer-men): followed the product link, inspected the image-led detail layout and expanded Ingredients. Detailed information sits behind disclosures beside the large product image.
- [Wegovy Pill](https://www.hims.com/weight-loss/wegovy-pill): inspected the opening product scene and the aligned comparison section. This was a design review; no medical claims were independently evaluated.
- The homepage’s testosterone product Learn more action navigated to `/testosterone`, which displayed Hims’s page-not-found view during this session. Other untested links should not be assumed to work.

The initial homepage quiz was opened without answering. No personal data, health answers, account creation, cart action or purchase was submitted. Product galleries, every menu branch, full intake flows and mobile variants were not exhaustively tested.

## Implications for Dawnstar

These are design judgments for the next visual revision, not changes already implemented:

1. Keep the company narrative based on Hims About. Use the main homepage to inform how Wellyn becomes tangible within that narrative.
2. Lead the Wellyn chapter with an original, generously sized service scene: a person’s everyday context, beautifully lit skincare objects and a restrained AI-selection overlay.
3. Let a short motion sequence express context → a smaller set of suitable options → a choice with a reason. The purpose is to explain the service without requiring interaction.
4. Keep the existing interactive comparison as a secondary example. Its current information density explains functionality but does not provide enough visual atmosphere on its own.
5. Strengthen product material, light, shadow and human use context. Avoid making the AI identity depend only on labels, dots or floating interface cards.
6. Preserve product discovery and purchase confidence as the central benefit; routine support follows selection. Show lifecycle wellness as company ambition.

Use original assets and visibly identified concept interfaces. Evidence-backed recommendation claims, real product data and actual purchase flows require the later service implementation.
