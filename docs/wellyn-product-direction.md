# Wellyn — founder direction, 2026-09-30

This note preserves the founder's input for future service building. It is not a launched feature list or a commitment to a launch schedule.

## Company and service

Dawnstar Corp is the company. Wellyn is its AI wellness service in development. The corporate website introduces the company's intention and gives a small, clearly illustrative glimpse of the first service. It should not become a shopping catalogue or a full product application.

## Lifecycle wellness

The founder's long-term vision spans life stages: from a child's lotion, through different adult routines and concerns, to older adults. People should be able to understand options, receive relevant product suggestions, make a purchase decision, and keep a routine that can change with their lives.

Age is one piece of context, alongside preferences, concerns, existing products, environment, budget and individual feedback. Do not reduce people to a gender, age bracket, skin tone or inferred identity. The initial product segment remains to be validated; the corporate image of three generations does not announce a child-facing service.

The useful product structure to explore later is **person / life stage → current concern → products already owned → explainable options → routine → feedback**. A family or caregiver account is a future research question, not an implemented capability. Any guidance for children would need its own product and safety work before launch.

## Initial focus retained

- US / English-speaking market.
- Skincare purchase decisions and routines as the first concrete use case.
- Reduce time spent choosing among many products; show understandable reasons for suggestions.
- Start from a person's existing shelf rather than requiring an entirely new set of purchases.
- Explore weather, seasonal preferences, morning/evening routines and check-ins.
- Product sales and affiliate revenue are the founder's intended commercial direction. Recommendation quality and commercial relationships need to remain understandable to users.
- Shopify with an AI layer is the intended later implementation direction; it is not part of this corporate-site build.

## Website expression

“Personal wellness guidance. Beginning with your skincare.” makes the first step concrete.

“Care for every chapter.” is a long-term company perspective, accompanied by explicit vision language. No live all-ages service, personalized assessment, medical treatment, professional endorsement, patent, research lab or outcome claim is made.

## Ideas still open

The founder mentioned a second idea and will provide it later. No substitute has been invented. Future discovery should decide the first audience, what product data can be trusted, what recommendation explanations users need, and how check-ins improve decisions over time.

## Research retained for the service build

## Founder clarification — product choice comes first

The core service is AI product discovery, curation and help deciding what to buy. Routine design and management follow that choice. The corporate website now reflects **Discover → Decide → Care**, with a product-comparison concept shown before the routine. The company identity, premium personal attention, access through AI, lifecycle wellness and the ambition for healthier, happier lives are explicit in the copy.
