## Where the findings belong

| Current Dawnstar company page | Future Wellyn product / service site |
| --- | --- |
| Company purpose, clear AI identity and development status | Catalog discovery, comparable attributes and evidence quality |
| One brief product-selection example | Multi-step preferences and ongoing personal context |

The cinematic-scene ideas in the Hims audit are optional art-direction inputs. They do not require adding every scene or category to Dawnstar. Full intake, checkout, clinician-led claims, testimonials and outcome numbers are not transferable.

Revisit the live sources before the service build: dated research does not establish current availability, prices, accuracy or performance. The direct browser audit also differs from earlier text-based research; retain that distinction when using the findings.
